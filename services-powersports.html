Add-ons like heavy mud removal, seat shampooing, or specialized metal/plastic polishing run an extra $25–$50. As a rule of thumb, a one-hour golf cart wash runs around $100, while a heavily mud-caked UTV needing three hours runs $250 or more.

Pricing Factors

What moves the price.

Vehicle Type & Size

A single-row golf cart, a double-row UTV, and a compact ATV all take different amounts of time and product to do right.

Mud & Grime Level

Off-road machines caked in thick mud need extra time, undercarriage washing, and heavier degreasing than a machine kept mostly clean.

Add-Ons

Heavy mud removal, seat shampooing, and specialized metal or plastic polishing are priced separately from the base wash or detail.
